Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 12, 2026

Summary: FitFlow stores all your personal fitness data on your device only. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information. We have no user accounts and no sign-in.

1. Introduction

FitFlow ("the App") is a home workout application developed by Shayona ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our iOS application.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. FitFlow is designed with a privacy-first approach — your personal fitness data never leaves your device.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

FitFlow does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Health or medical records
  • Biometric data
  • Browsing or search history
  • Contact lists or address books
  • Photos, videos, or camera access
  • Device identifiers for tracking purposes

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

FitFlow stores the following data locally on your device only using Apple's standard on-device storage (UserDefaults):

  • Fitness preferences: Your chosen fitness level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced) and weekly workout goal
  • Display name: An optional name you provide during onboarding, stored only on your device
  • Workout history: Records of completed workouts including date, duration, and estimated calories burned
  • Custom workouts: Any workouts you create within the app
  • Fitness plan progress: Your progress through structured workout plans
  • Achievement data: Milestones and achievements you have unlocked
  • App preferences: Notification reminder times and settings

This data is never transmitted to our servers or any third party. If you delete the app, all local data is permanently removed.

4. Health and Fitness Data

FitFlow is a fitness and exercise application. We take the handling of health-related data seriously:

  • FitFlow does not integrate with Apple HealthKit
  • FitFlow does not access or read any data from the Health app
  • Calorie estimates provided by FitFlow are approximations for informational purposes only and should not be considered medical advice
  • All fitness data (workout history, progress) is stored exclusively on your device
  • We do not use your fitness data for advertising, analytics, or profiling
Medical Disclaimer: FitFlow is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. The workouts, calorie estimates, and fitness information provided are for general fitness purposes only. Always consult a healthcare professional before starting any new exercise program, especially if you have pre-existing health conditions.

5. Third-Party Services

FitFlow uses the following third-party services with limited scope:

a) Firebase (Google)

  • Firebase Firestore: Used in read-only mode to deliver the workout exercise library to the app. No personal data is written to or stored in Firebase.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Collects anonymous crash reports to help us fix bugs and improve app stability. Crash data does not contain personal information and cannot be used to identify you.
  • Firebase Analytics: Collects anonymous, aggregated usage data (e.g., app opens, screen views) to help us understand how the app is used. This data is not linked to your identity.

For more information, see Firebase Privacy Policy.

b) Apple (StoreKit)

  • In-app subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We do not have access to your payment information, Apple ID, or billing details.
  • For information on how Apple handles your purchase data, see Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Notifications

FitFlow may request permission to send local notifications for workout reminders. These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device and do not involve any server communication. You can disable notifications at any time through your device's Settings.

7. Children's Privacy

FitFlow is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since all data is stored locally on the device and we have no user accounts or data collection, there is no mechanism for collecting children's data.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

  • All personal fitness data is stored on your device only
  • To delete all your data, simply delete the FitFlow app from your device
  • Anonymous crash reports and analytics data retained by Firebase are not linked to your identity and follow Google's data retention policies

9. Data Security

Your fitness data is protected by your device's built-in security features, including device passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID. Since data is stored locally and not transmitted over the internet, it is not exposed to network-based threats.

10. Your Rights

Since FitFlow does not collect or store personal data on external servers, traditional data access requests do not apply. You have full control over your data on your device at all times. You can delete all data by uninstalling the app.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
